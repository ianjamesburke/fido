Why Fido

Built Different

⚡

Lightning Fast

Terminal-native UI with zero overhead. Renders in microseconds. No browser, no Electron, no garbage.

⌨

Keyboard-Driven

Every action on a keybind. Vim-style navigation throughout. Your hands never leave the keyboard.

🔒

Privacy-First

No algorithms surfacing content you didn't ask for. No ads. No tracking. Just signal, no noise.

🦀

Built with Rust

Memory-safe, blazingly fast, compiled to a single binary. Ships and runs everywhere without dependencies.

  • Direct messaging
  • Follow hashtags

  • Upvote / downvote posts
  • Customizable color schemes
  • Configurable sorting and display
